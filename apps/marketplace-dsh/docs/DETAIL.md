# Marketplace

Every Automagik app in one place: the Bot team and the welcome that ships with the app, the add-ons you can install (Omni, Genie, ACP agents), and the Bring your own AI providers Automagik vetted.

## Use this app

The **Apps** entry in the sidebar opens the marketplace. What is included shows as included; everything else can be installed and updated from there, one app at a time, at exactly the version the Automagik market lists.

This is the first development version: the Apps entry and its page are live, and the catalog, installs and updates land here next.
